# Adjacent alternatives

Date: 9 October 2026.

## Decision: no adjacent alternative is recommended at this stage

The brief asks for up to two adjacent questions **if neither E-N01 nor G-P2 can be redesigned into a promising primary thesis**. That condition is not met. Both projects have a redesign whose decisive uncertainty can be resolved by a pilot of a few hours:
- E-R1 depends on N-CEN prevalence;
- G-R1 depends on the Credit Access match rate and delinquency event counts.

Proposing new topics before those pilots run would spread effort without information.

I nevertheless screened candidate adjacent questions. None cleared the bar of a verified literature gap, verified public data and a defined design. They are recorded here so they are not re-proposed without new evidence.

## Candidates screened and rejected

| **A. Fee-waiver unwind as a monetary-transmission friction in money-market funds** (2022 tightening). Did the restoration of waived fees, or recapture, delay pass-through of policy rates to fund investors? | SEC N-MFP (fund-level gross and net yields), ICI aggregates | Di Maggio & Kacperczyk (2017) on waivers at the ZLB; Xiao (2020, RFS) on MMF pass-through; NY Fed analysis shows MMF yields rose by about 97% of the EFFR increase since March 2022 (secondary) | The effect is front-loaded into the first 25–50 bp of the cycle, so it is economically small against more than 400 bp of tightening. SCE "delinquency expectations" as a macro-financial leading indicator** (aggregate Q30 vs bureau delinquency rates) | SCE aggregates; NY Fed Household Debt and Credit Report | Fed notes on forecasting card delinquency exist (secondary) | The aggregate version is a time-series exercise with about 150 monthly observations and heavy competition from bureau-based forecasts. The individual-level version is already G-R3. **Reject as separate** |

## If both pilots fail

Begin a new primary-thesis search with the same screening rules:

1. a verified gap (full texts of the 3–5 closest papers read);
2. a public dataset whose decisive fields are inspected *before* design work;
3. a mechanism-derived benchmark effect size;
4. a 4–8 hour decisive pilot defined in advance.

Concentrate the search where this review found under-used public data:
- **N-CEN** fund-level contract and service-provider fields (beyond securities lending, which is occupied);
- **SCE modules** linked by `userid` (Credit Access, Household Finance).

Novelty must be re-checked from scratch; this list is not an endorsement.
